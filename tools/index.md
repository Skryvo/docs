# Agent tools

> Every tool an agent can call, with its inputs, an example and its cost.

Every tool an agent can call, with its inputs, an example and its cost.

- [List brands (`list_brands`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-brands/): Your brands and which one is the default.
- [List channels (`list_channels`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-channels/): Your connected accounts and blog sites, and which brands use each.
- [Write a post (`write_post`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-post/): Drafts a post for up to four platforms in the brand's voice. One generation covers them all. Nothing is scheduled.
- [Design a picture (`design_picture`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/design-picture/): Designs a picture for the post in your brand's look, at each platform's own size, using Image Studio credits.
- [Job status (`job_status`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/job-status/): Says where a slow post or picture is, and hands back its answer once it is done.
- [Post results (`post_results`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/post-results/): Reach and engagement, best posting times and top posts for the days you ask about.
- [Schedule a post (`schedule_post`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/schedule-post/): Puts a post on your calendar for each chosen account, with its picture. Skryvo publishes it on the quarter hour and checks it went live.
- [List scheduled posts (`list_scheduled_posts`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-scheduled-posts/): Upcoming and recent posts with their status and any publishing problem.
- [Cancel a post (`cancel_post`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/cancel-post/): Removes a scheduled post before it goes out.
- [Brand design (`brand_design`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/brand-design/): Your brand's logo, colours and fonts, and the look every new post is designed in.
- [Change the look (`change_look`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/change-look/): Designs your look again bolder, calmer, closer to your site or with another photo, or keeps the one on show.
- [Plan an article (`plan_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/plan-article/): Researches and plans an article: headlines, search phrase, outline, sources and questions only the author can answer. Free; one of the month's plans.
- [Write an article (`write_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-article/): Writes the planned article in the background, researched, fact-checked and scored as in the app.
- [Article status (`article_status`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/article-status/): Says where an article is, and hands back the finished text with its score and every checked claim with its sources.
- [Article results (`article_results`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/article-results/): Shows where a published article went and how it does, with its Google search numbers.
- [Article SEO (`article_seo`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/article-seo/): Checks an article's SEO for free: score, search phrase and every issue the editor's SEO card shows.
- [Improve an article (`improve_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/improve-article/): Fixes those issues, adds an FAQ citing its sources or rewrites only disputed sentences, saved as a new version.
- [Import an article (`import_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/import-article/): Brings your own article in from text or a public address, once you say it is yours to publish. Free, within your plan's monthly limit.
- [Turn an article into posts (`repurpose_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/repurpose-article/): Turns a finished article into one post per platform, ready to schedule.
- [Publish an article (`publish_article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/publish-article/): Schedules a finished article to a connected blog, or sends it there as a draft.
- [Write an email (`write_email`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-email/): Writes a marketing email in the brand's voice.
- [Write a product listing (`write_listing`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-listing/): Writes a product listing shaped for its marketplace's rules.
- [Balance (`balance`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/balance/): Says what your plan still allows this month.
- [Keep as defaults (`keep_defaults`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/keep-defaults/): Keeps your choices as the brand's defaults, after you say yes.
- [List platforms (`list_platforms`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-platforms/): Every channel Skryvo offers, connected or not, and what each one takes.
- [Change a post (`change_post`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/change-post/): Moves a scheduled post that has not gone out, changes its words, or both.
- [Review a post (`review_post`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/review-post/): Records your decision on a team post waiting for approval.
- [Plan the calendar (`plan_calendar`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/plan-calendar/): Plans a week or a month of social posts for a brand, using one generation. Adds nothing yet.
- [Accept a plan (`accept_plan`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/accept-plan/): Adds the plan rows you keep to your calendar as planned topics. Nothing is published.
- [Write ad copy (`write_ad`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-ad/): Writes ad copy in the brand's voice, shaped for each ad platform. No ad is run.
- [Find content (`find_content`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/find-content/): What you already made, newest first, with the ids the other tools take.
- [Add a brand (`add_brand`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/add-brand/): Adds a brand and reads its voice, kit and facts from its website, using one voice training.
- [Add a video (`add_video`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/add-video/): Opens an upload for your own video file. Skryvo never makes video.
- [Finish a video (`finish_video`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/finish-video/): Reads an uploaded video and makes it ready to schedule.
- [List videos (`list_videos`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-videos/): Your own finished videos, uploaded here or in Skryvo.
