# The words Skryvo uses

> Brand, voice, look, piece, channel, generation, credit and the other words used across the app and docs.

| Word | What it means in Skryvo |
|---|---|
| Brand | One business or person Skryvo writes for. A brand holds its voice, its kit (logo, colours, fonts), its look, its own photos, its facts, its channels and its calendar. Plans set how many brands an account holds. |
| Voice | How a brand writes: a style guide, its terms and approved examples, read from the website and the writing samples you add. Every writer uses it. A voice with no examples says so instead of guessing. |
| Kit | The brand's logo, colour roles and fonts, read from its site and editable under Brands › Design. |
| Look | The design every picture for a brand is made in: picture style, layout, where the title and logo sit. It is shown as three designed posts. |
| Kept look | The look you chose with **Use this look** (or an agent's [`change_look`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/change-look/) with `keep`). Pictures are designed in it until you change it. |
| Piece | One thing Skryvo made or you brought in: a post, an article, an email, an ad or a listing. **For this piece** steers the pictures of that one piece only. |
| Channel | An account Skryvo publishes to: a LinkedIn profile, an Instagram account, a WordPress site, a Telegram channel. One sign-in can connect several, such as several Facebook Pages. |
| Generation | One AI writing request, counted against the month's allowance. A batch of posts for several platforms is one generation; a researched article is 2. Writing your own words uses none. |
| Image credit | What a picture costs. A costlier model or a larger size takes more credits, and the price shows before you generate. |
| Calendar | Where every post, article and planned topic sits with its date and time. Skryvo publishes from it. |
| Planned topic | A calendar row with a topic and a time but no post yet. Skryvo drafts it before its time for you to approve. |
| Desk | The dashboard's list of what needs you today: drafts to approve, posts that need a channel connected, posts a platform refused. |
| Plan | Hobby, Pro or Enterprise: what an account may make each month. See [Plans and the trial](https://skryvo.ai/docs/start/plans-and-trial/). |
| Trial | The first 7 days of Hobby or Pro, with no card. |
| Workspace | An owner's account and everything in it. On Pro and Enterprise the owner can invite members, who work in the workspace on its plan. See [Teams](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/teams/). |
| Read back | The check after publishing: Skryvo asks the platform for the post it just sent, and only a post found there counts as published. |
