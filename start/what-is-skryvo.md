# What Skryvo is

> One app that learns a brand once, then writes, checks, designs, schedules and publishes its content.

Skryvo is an AI content platform for founders, marketers and agencies. It learns a brand once (its voice, its look, its photos and its facts), then writes social posts, blog articles, emails, ad copy and product listings in that voice, designs their pictures, puts posts on one calendar and publishes them to the accounts the person connected. Everything it does in the app, an AI agent such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can do too, and so can a script through the REST API.

## What it does

| Part | What happens |
|---|---|
| Brand | Skryvo reads the brand's website and the social accounts you add, and builds a voice, a design kit and a set of facts you can see and edit. See [Brands and voice](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/brands-and-voice/). |
| Writing | One request drafts a post for each platform you pick, rewritten for that channel. Articles start from live search results, cite their sources and have their claims checked. See [Posts](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/posts/) and [Articles](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/articles/). |
| Checking | Each cited sentence in an article is checked against its source and given a verdict. See [How a claim is checked](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/). |
| Pictures | Pictures are designed in the brand's kept look, at each platform's size, with the brand's real logo and photos placed by code. See [Pictures](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/pictures/) and [The Studio](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/studio/). |
| Calendar | Every post, article and planned topic sits on one calendar. Posts go out on the quarter hour. See [The calendar](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/calendar/). |
| Publishing | Skryvo publishes through each platform's own API, then reads the post back to confirm it is live. See [Publishing and the live check](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/publishing/). |
| Results | Each post's numbers are read at 1 hour, 24 hours and 7, 30, 60 and 90 days. See [Results](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/results/). |

You can also write your own posts (no AI and no generation used), bring your own article in from a file or a link ([Bring your own article](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/import-article/)), and steer the pictures of one piece.

## Where it publishes

Live today: LinkedIn, X, Facebook Page, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, Slack, WordPress, WordPress.com, Dev.to, YouTube and Google Business Profile. Built and waiting for the platform's approval: LinkedIn Page. Each channel has its own page under [Channels](https://skryvo.ai/docs/channels/).

## Three ways to use it

1. **The app** at https://app.skryvo.ai, in 20 languages.
2. **An AI agent.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or any MCP client to `https://api.skryvo.ai/mcp`. The agent gets 34 tools and shows you each post before it schedules it. See [Connect an agent](https://skryvo.ai/docs/agents/connect/).
3. **The REST API** at `https://api.skryvo.ai/api/v1/agent`, one route per tool, with an API key. See the [API overview](https://skryvo.ai/docs/api/overview/).

## What it does not do

Skryvo makes no AI video: a post can carry a video you upload, and Skryvo writes the caption, title and timing around it. It has no shared inbox and no social listening. The full list is on [Known limits](https://skryvo.ai/docs/trust/known-limits/).
