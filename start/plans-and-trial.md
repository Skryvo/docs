# Plans and the 7-day trial

> What Hobby, Pro and Enterprise include each month, how the trial with no card works, and what a call costs.

Prices are in US dollars. Every plan has every channel, the AI agent tools and the REST API.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Generations a month | Image credits | Brand voices | Scheduled posts waiting at once | X posts a month | Article imports a month | Webhook endpoints |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hobby | $15 | $149 | 150 | 75 | 2 | 20 | 30 | 50 | 3 |
| Pro | $49 | $490 | 500 | 300 | 5 | 100 | 100 | 200 | 10 |
| Enterprise | $499 | $4,990 | 5,000 | 3,000 | 10 | Unlimited | 1,500 | 500 | 25 |

- A researched blog article uses 2 generations. A batch of social posts for several platforms uses one.
- Article plans are free: a month holds as many as the plan has generations, and a failed plan is given back.
- On X, a post with a link counts as 10 posts, because X charges Skryvo far more for it. At the limit, X posts stop with a message and the other channels keep posting.
- The humanizer: 25 a month on Hobby, unlimited on Pro and unlimited on Enterprise.
- Teams come with Pro and Enterprise: invite as many people as you need, with no seats to buy. Usage stays on the owner's plan. See [Teams](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/teams/).
- Paying yearly costs about ten months of the monthly price.

## The 7-day free trial

Hobby and Pro start with a 7-day free trial that costs $0 and needs no card. There is no free plan.

1. Pick Hobby or Pro in setup or in Plan & billing, press **Start for $0** and start making at once.
2. For 7 days you get a week's share of the plan: 35 generations and 20 image credits on Hobby, 120 generations and 70 image credits on Pro. Every other limit is the plan's own.
3. The day before the trial ends, Skryvo emails you the date and the amount.
4. Add a card in Billing to keep the plan from day 8, when the full month opens. **Start my plan now** charges the card and opens the full month at once.
5. Without a card the trial ends and nothing is charged. The account becomes read-only: you can still see and export everything, and posts scheduled after the trial wait until a card is added.

A trial is for an account that has never had one, and a social account can be connected to only one account's trial.

## Moving allowance

On Hobby, Pro and Enterprise, this month's unused allowance moves either way in Plan & billing: 2 generations make 1 image credit, and 1 image credit turns back into 2 generations, up to 10 moves a month. Moving starts with the first paid month, not during the trial. An image top-up is 100 credits for $8, and credits bought as a pack stay image credits.

## What a call costs

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| A batch of social posts, one per platform | 1 generation |
| A researched article | 2 generations, plus image credits for its pictures; a failed article is not charged |
| An article plan | Free, one of the month's plans |
| An email, ad copy or a product listing | 1 generation each |
| A picture | Its image credits, shown before you generate |
| Your own post or your own article brought in | No generation |
| Scheduling, publishing and reading results | Nothing |

The [`balance`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/balance/) tool, or Plan & billing in the app, says what this month still allows. Cancel any time from billing settings: the plan stays active to the end of the period you paid for. Full pricing questions: [Pricing](https://skryvo.ai/pricing/).
