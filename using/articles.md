# Researched articles

> Plan an article from live search results, answer what only you know, then let Skryvo write, check and score it.

The **Blog** page writes a researched article in the brand's voice: it reads what already ranks for the topic, plans an outline, writes it, checks its claims against their sources and scores it.

## Plan, then write

1. Open **Blog** and give the topic, the length, the style (how-to, listicle, comparison, case study, explainer and others) and who it is for.
2. Skryvo plans it. The plan holds the headline and other headlines, the search phrase it aims at with its monthly searches, the outline, its sources with the facts found in them, and up to three questions only you can answer.
3. Answer the questions in your own words, pick a headline and say whether you want a cover picture and up to 3 more inside.
4. Write. The article streams in, section by section, then the checks run.

A plan is free: it uses one of the month's article plans, and a failed plan is given back. Writing the article uses 2 generations, plus image credits for its pictures. A failed article is not charged.

## How the research works

Searches run in the readers' market and language. Skryvo reads up to ten full pages, at most two from any one site, and follows their links to primary sources. Government, university, standards and medical bodies rank highest. Each page becomes a few facts with their source and year; a fact three or more years old (one year for news) is marked stale, and the sentence that uses it says its year.

The writer is told to type no figure or link it was not handed. A figure no source states is removed before you see the draft. Each cited sentence is then checked against its source. [How a claim is checked](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/) explains the verdicts.

## The score

The article score has six weighted parts and no AI-detection or human-likelihood number. The **SEO** check is free and lists each issue: the phrase missing from the title, no meta description, no links to sources, the heading structure, the length. **Improve** fixes them and saves a new version; an article's first improvement is free and each one after uses one generation.

## Copy, export and publish

Copy puts formatted text and plain text on the clipboard. Export offers PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown and plain text. To put it on a blog, see [Publishing](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/publishing/).

## When something goes wrong

- **A claim reads Unverified or Disputed.** It stays in the draft, marked, for you to decide. See [How a claim is checked](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/).
- **The research found little.** A page that does not load in time is skipped. Add the pages you trust as sources in the brief.
- **A new length, style or language.** These need a new plan, because the outline was built for them.
