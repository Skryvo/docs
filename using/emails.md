# Marketing emails

> Write a marketing email in the brand's voice, with subject lines, a preheader and a Send-Ready score.

The **Email** page writes a marketing email in the brand's voice: subject lines, a preheader, the body and a call to action, with HTML ready to paste into your email tool. Skryvo writes the email; it does not send it.

## Write one

1. Open **Email** and pick the type: welcome, promotional, newsletter, follow-up, re-engagement, transactional, announcement or abandoned cart. Or use reply mode: paste the email you received and say what you want to answer.
2. Say what it promotes or says, and to whom. Add the audience, tone, language, how many subject lines, the call to action and its link, and the length.
3. Add merge tags such as `{{firstName}}` and `{{companyName}}` where you want them.
4. Generate. One email uses one generation, given back if the run fails.

## The Send-Ready score

| Part | Weight | What it checks |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverability | 40% | Spam triggers, shouting, subject and preheader length, the number of links |
| Specificity | 35% | Concrete details from your brief, against vague phrases |
| Honesty | 25% | Figures, links, urgency and superlatives your brief does not back |

An email reads ready at 85 or more with no serious finding. Each finding has a fix: the simple ones are free and apply together; a rewrite of one passage is free three times an email, then one generation each. A rewrite that would add a claim is refused.

Claims your brief does not back, such as a figure or a deadline, are removed from the subject lines, preheader and body before you see them, and listed.

## Fonts

An email is set in the brand's fonts when its kit has them.

## When something goes wrong

- **A detail you did not give.** The writer can add a small detail the brief did not hold. Read the email before you send it.
- **The run failed.** The generation is given back.
