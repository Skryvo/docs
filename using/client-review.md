# Client review from a link

> Send a client a link to approve, ask for changes on, or comment on posts, with no account and no sign-in.

A client can answer posts from a page with no sign-in. You choose the posts, make the link and send it yourself.

## Share posts with a client

1. Choose the posts: tick waiting posts on **Approvals** and choose **Share with client**, or use **Share with client** on a post in the calendar.
2. A link holds 1 to 30 drafted, unpublished posts of one brand. Mixing brands is refused.
3. Pick how long it lasts: 1, 3, 7 or 14 days (7 is picked).
4. The link is copied. Send it to your client by email or chat.

The owner, admins, reviewers and editors can share, each only for brands they reach.

## What the client sees and does

The page shows the brand's name, logo and colour and the posts. It never shows a member's name or email, a role or the plan. For each post the client can:

- **Approve** it,
- **Ask for changes**, with a note, or
- **Comment**, which decides nothing: up to 500 characters, 20 comments a post.

One answer a round: once a post is decided it reads as settled. When the author sends it for approval again, the same link takes a new answer.

## What an answer does

| Who made the link | What the client's answer does |
|---|---|
| Someone who decides approvals (owner, admin, reviewer) | It is the team's decision: the post goes out at its time, or is held with the note |
| An editor, on a brand that needs approval | It is kept beside the post: "Client approved, waiting for a reviewer"; a reviewer still decides |
| Anyone, on a post outside approval | A yes clears it; a no holds it with the client's words |

Every answer reaches the link's maker and the post's author in the app and by email, with a link to the post's day in the calendar.

## When a link ends

A link ends when its maker ends it, when it expires, when its maker leaves the workspace or loses the brand, or when the team's plan lapses. An ended link says so and takes no answer. **Your client links** on Approvals lists your links; a brand's page lists all of its links for admins and the owner.

## From an agent

Client links are made in the app. An agent reads a post's state with [`list_scheduled_posts`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-scheduled-posts/).
