# The Studio editor

> Edit any picture as layers: words, logo, photos and sizes, with AI changes priced before they run.

The **Studio** opens any picture Skryvo made, or one you bring, as a design with layers, pages and versions. It is never a blank canvas: it opens knowing what the picture is for, so its size, its first layout and its "Use in …" button already fit.

## Open it

- **Edit** on any picture in a post, an article, an email or a listing.
- **Design one** on an empty picture slot.
- A tile in the **Library**.
- **Edit in Studio** on a piece.

## Find your way around

| Area | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Top bar | The name, undo and redo, versions, **Preview** and **Download** |
| Rail | Styles · Brand · Photos · Text · Elements · AI · Sizes · Checks; one click opens a panel, a second puts it away |
| Selection strip | The tools for what you selected, or the next suggested step when nothing is |
| Ask bar | One line at the foot of the page: say a change in words |

**Sizes** lists the page's own place first, then the places the brand shows up. A size can follow the main page or have its own layout. **Checks** marks a problem, such as words too small or too close to the edge, and offers its fix.

On a phone, the rail becomes a strip of Text · Elements · Photos · Brand · More, and panels open as sheets.

## What costs what

Most edits are free: moving, resizing, text, fonts, colours, the brand's logo and photos, sizes and checks. Changes that make new pixels use image credits, and the price shows before you apply them:

| Change | Image credits |
|---|---|
| Change, remove or replace an area | 1 |
| **Remove the background** | 1 |
| **Make it 2K/4K** | 2 |
| Describe a change in words | 2 to 6, by the model |

A failed change is given back. Each result is kept as a take on the layer, so you can step back to an earlier one for free.

**Design more styles** lays out up to three new post styles for the brand, free. A request in the ask bar for a new look returns three styles in the brand to pick from, also free. **Save as style** keeps a design for the brand to reuse; a brand keeps up to 12 of its own designs, used for layout and style only.

## Download and use

**Download** offers PNG, JPG, PNG at 2×, a see-through PNG, PDF, every page as a zip, and a 6-second video in which the layers slide into place, to post as a video. **Use in …** hands the design back to the post or article it came from.

Every save keeps a version; the save state in the top bar opens them.

## When something goes wrong

- **A save failed.** The save state says so and retries when you choose it.
- **An AI change looks wrong.** Undo, or open the versions and go back.

## From an agent

An agent designs pictures with [`design_picture`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/design-picture/); editing one in the Studio happens in the app.
