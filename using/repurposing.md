# Repurposing a piece

> Turn an article or post into native posts for other platforms and a newsletter, in the brand's voice.

**Repurpose** turns a finished piece, such as an article, into posts for other platforms and an email newsletter, each written for its own place in the brand's voice and keeping the piece's facts.

## Repurpose a piece

1. Open the piece and choose **Repurpose**.
2. Pick the targets: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads, Pinterest or an email newsletter. Ones already written for this piece are listed under "Already written".
3. With two or more brands, **Write as** picks the brand.
4. The button names how many platforms it writes for. Each result opens in the composer like any post, ready to schedule.

Your own article in the editor offers the same as **Make posts**.

## Limits and cost

A job uses one generation for all its platforms, given back if the job fails.

| | Hobby | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platforms per job | 3 | 5 | 6 |
| Jobs a month | 25 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

## Without asking each time

**Automations › After posting › New article → social drafts** repurposes each new article once, into a reminder post for each connected account at its next free time, for one generation. It also reads the brand's own site feed, at most every 6 hours, and repurposes up to two new articles from the last 3 days that Skryvo did not publish, with the article's address in each post.

## When something goes wrong

- **More platforms than the plan allows.** Pick fewer, or run a second job.
- **The job failed.** The generation is given back.

## From an agent

[`repurpose_article`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/repurpose-article/) turns a finished article into one post per platform for [`schedule_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/schedule-post/).
