# Approvals

> When a brand's posts need approval, editors' posts wait for a reviewer, an admin or the owner to approve them.

Approvals let a team agree on a post before it goes out. They are part of [Teams](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/teams/), on Pro and Enterprise.

## Turn it on

Open the brand's page and switch on **Posts need approval**. Only admins and the owner see the switch. It is off by default.

## What happens to a post

1. An editor schedules or changes a post on that brand. It is held, and the calendar shows it as a draft: **Waiting for approval**.
2. The owner, admins and reviewers who reach the brand hear about it in the app and by email, and see it on **Approvals** in the sidebar, which carries a dot while something waits.
3. They read the post's text, platform, time, brand and who sent it, then choose:
   - **Approve**: it goes out at its time and the author is told.
   - **Ask for changes**: a note is required. The post is held as **Changes asked**, and once fixed, the author presses **Send for approval** and it waits again.
   - **Edit and approve** (admins and the owner): saves the edited words on the post and approves it in one step.

A post is decided once; a second decision is refused. Nothing waiting or sent back is published, whether by the schedule or by publish now. Posts by admins and the owner go straight out.

## Who can do what

| Role | On a brand that needs approval |
|---|---|
| Owner, Admin | Write, schedule, approve, ask for changes, edit and approve |
| Editor | Write and schedule; their posts wait |
| Reviewer | Read and decide; change nothing else |

## Notices

Under **Settings › Notifications › Team**, each person picks how far each team notice reaches them: in Skryvo and by email (the default), in Skryvo only, or off. Off still leaves the queue on Approvals.

## Clients

To have a client approve posts from a link without an account, see [Client review](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/client-review/).

## From an agent

An editor's agent that schedules on such a brand creates a waiting post, because the rule lives in the calendar itself. [`review_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/review-post/) records a reviewer's, an admin's or the owner's decision.
