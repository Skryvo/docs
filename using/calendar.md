# The calendar

> Plan, move and repeat posts on one calendar in your time zone, with posting times, previews and an AI plan.

The **Calendar** holds every post and article you schedule, for every brand and channel, in month, week and day views in your device's time zone.

## Add a post

- Choose **+ Add** on a day or an empty time slot, or **Create post**. A drawer opens with a short brief: platform, topic, preset and campaign.
- **Write with AI · 1 credit** writes the posts in the drawer, in the brand's voice and look. **Write it myself** opens a plain editor held to the strictest platform's limit.
- **Plan your first week**, on an empty calendar, offers an AI plan.

## Move, hold and repeat

- **Drag** a post to a new day and quarter hour; every move offers Undo. On a phone, press and hold first.
- **Move** in a post's details picks a date, then a time.
- **Hold it** pauses a post.
- **Bring it back** repeats a post every 1 to 12 weeks, 1 to 12 times. Each repeat is rewritten, for one generation, so it never goes out word for word again; a take too close to the original is held.
- **Duplicate** makes a fresh copy at a date and time you pick. **Unschedule** removes the post from the calendar and keeps the post.

Posts go out on the quarter hour: :00, :15, :30 or :45. A time between moves to the next one.

## Posting times

Each platform keeps up to four usual posting times. A new post takes the first one still ahead that day and not yet used on that platform. The [Performance](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/results/) page can put your best measured hour first.

## Plan a week or a month

The AI plan writes a week or a month of topics for the brand, one generation, each row with its date, time, platform, topic and why. Nothing is added until you accept the rows you keep, as planned topics.

## Share a preview

**Preview link** makes a link to one post that shows it as its feed will, for 30 days, with no account needed. To ask a client to approve posts, see [Client review](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/client-review/).

## The feed

For Instagram, **Feed** shows the brand's grid as followers will see it: what goes out next above what is live. Drag to swap two posts' times. Its check names blank squares, gaps over a week and two posts on one day.

## States you will see

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not set to go out yet |
| Scheduled | Set to go out at its time |
| Going out now | Locked two minutes before its time; edits wait |
| Posted | Out, with its link |
| Didn't post | It could not go out; the reason and **Try again** are on the post |

## When something goes wrong

- **The time zone changed.** Scheduled posts keep their clock time in the new zone.
- **Upgrade to schedule.** Scheduling needs a plan; the trial includes it.

## From an agent

[`plan_calendar`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/plan-calendar/) plans the rows and [`accept_plan`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/accept-plan/) adds the ones the person keeps. [`list_scheduled_posts`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/list-scheduled-posts/), [`change_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/change-post/) and [`cancel_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/cancel-post/) work on what is there.
