# AI answers about your brand

> See whether AI answer engines name your brand to buyers, who they name instead, and which of your facts they get wrong.

**Brands › AI answers** checks what AI answer engines tell your buyers: whether they name your brand, who they name instead, which pages they read, and which of your Brand Book facts they get wrong.

## How a check works

1. Skryvo drafts the questions your buyers ask, most of them without your brand's name. You keep or edit them.
2. Each question is asked on ChatGPT search, Google AI Mode and Perplexity, twice each, because answers change from one run to the next.
3. Each answer is read for whether it names your brand, which companies it names instead, and any statement that contradicts a fact in your Brand Book, quoted.
4. The panel shows how many answers named you, the names offered instead, the sources read where you were left out, and one fix to do first.

A fix is planned, not written: it adds a calendar item for tomorrow, a post that states the fact or an answer page for the question, which you write through the usual writer and its claim check.

## Plans

| Plan | Questions | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Hobby | 10 | Every 30 days |
| Pro | 30 | Every 7 days |
| Enterprise | 100 | Every 7 days |

Questions are counted across all the account's brands, so more brands do not multiply the questions. The check is part of the plan, with no price of its own.

## When something goes wrong

- **An engine reads "not checked".** It failed to answer that time; the next check asks again.
- **A wrong fact keeps coming back.** Make sure the fact is on your own site in plain words, then publish the planned fix.
