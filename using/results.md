# Results and best times

> How Skryvo reads each published post at fixed checkpoints, finds what worked and suggests when to post.

The **Performance** page reports how your published posts and articles did: reach, engagement rate, clicks and posts measured, a trend, each channel, best times, each post and search.

## When numbers are read

Each post published through Skryvo is read from its platform at 1 hour, 24 hours, 7, 30, 60 and 90 days. A post you posted yourself counts too once you mark it **I posted it** with its link and a connected account can read it back.

| Channel | What is read |
|---|---|
| X, Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest | The platform's own numbers for the post |
| Bluesky | Likes, replies, reposts and quotes; Bluesky gives no reach |
| Blog articles | Search clicks, impressions, click rate and position from your Google Search Console, once you **Connect Google** |
| Telegram, Discord, Slack | Nothing: these platforms give apps no numbers, so their posts read as unmeasured |

A number Skryvo cannot read is never shown as a zero: it reads as pending, reconnect, unmeasured or unlinked.

Links in posts to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Threads, Mastodon and Pinterest carry tracking tags, so visits to your site can be matched to the post.

## Findings

Findings are computed from your own posts, never written by AI: whether a question opening, a length, a weekday, a time of day or a channel did better. A finding needs five posts on each side and a 30% gap; the strongest three show, each with the posts behind it and an action such as **Write 3 like this**.

## Best times

For each channel, the hour with the best engagement rate among its own posts, once it has 8 or more posts and 3 at that hour. Applying them makes the calendar start new posts at those hours.

## Share and export

On a paid plan, **Share report** makes a link to one brand's results for a client, for 30 days, which you can withdraw. Posts export as CSV.

Each piece also has a Results tab in the editor with every place it went out.

## When something goes wrong

- **Nothing measured yet.** The page lists what stands between you and the first reading: a connected account, a post published through Skryvo, and the first reading about an hour later.
- **Reconnect.** The account's access ended or lacks a permission; reconnect it in Connected Accounts.

## From an agent

[`post_results`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/post-results/) returns the report for 7, 30 or 90 days. [`article_results`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/article-results/) reports one article.
