# Brands and their voice

> How Skryvo reads a brand once, its voice, look and facts, and how every piece is then written in it.

A brand is everything Skryvo knows about one business: how it writes, how it looks, what it may say about itself and which accounts post for it. Every piece you make belongs to one brand, and the brand switcher in the sidebar picks the one you work in.

## What a brand holds

| Part | What it is | Where you change it |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | A style guide, its words and a few approved samples of the brand's own writing | Brands › Voice |
| Look | Logo, colours, fonts, the brand's own photos and the look its pictures are designed in | Brands › Look |
| Knowledge | Facts, offers and questions the brand answers, each with its source | Brands › Voice › Knowledge |
| Rules and facts | When AI may make pictures, reviews shared with permission and figures with their source | Brands › Look › Rules and facts |
| Channels | Which of your connected accounts post for this brand | Brands › Channels |

## Set up a brand

1. Open **Settings › Brands** and choose **Add a brand**. Your first brand is set up during sign-up the same way.
2. Give its website. Skryvo reads the site: its logo, colours, fonts, photos, the social accounts it links to and how it writes. No website yet? Give its social accounts, upload a logo, photos, past designs or past writing, or start from a look.
3. Check what it found. Keep or refresh each part of the brand card, and say who is in the photos.
4. Keep a look. Skryvo designs the brand's first posts in a few directions; the one you keep is the look every new picture wears.
5. Pick which connected accounts post for the brand under **Channels**.

You can leave while it reads ("Carry on, I'll check it later"); the brand appears in the sidebar when it is ready.

## How the voice is learned

Adding writing samples runs one close read of them. It writes the style guide (how the brand opens and closes, what it avoids, the phrases it uses), its words, and keeps up to 8 approved samples. A social post leads with the brand's own posts on the platform it is written for, so a LinkedIn post learns from the brand's LinkedIn posts first.

**Voice strength** on the brand's page is measured from the samples themselves: one consistent author scores higher than a mix of writers. A voice needs at least one approved sample to count as trained. Each piece then shows whether the voice wrote it.

Edits you make to drafts can become suggestions for the voice. They show three at a time, with **Add all**.

## When something goes wrong

- **The site could not be read.** Some sites block readers or load their words by script. Give the brand's social accounts or upload past writing instead.
- **The voice sounds generic.** Add more of the brand's own writing, from one author where you can, and approve it as samples.
- **A setup failed.** The board says so and offers **Try again**.
