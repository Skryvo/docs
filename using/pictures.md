# Pictures and image credits

> How Skryvo designs pictures in a brand's kept look, what they cost in image credits, and how to steer one piece.

Pictures in Skryvo are made for their place: a post's picture at that platform's size, an article's header and inside pictures, an email header, a listing's gallery. Each is designed in the brand's kept look, with its real logo and, where the brand has them, its own photos.

## How a picture is made

1. Skryvo plans the picture's place: its size, its words and where the logo goes.
2. It paints a scene with no words, or uses one of the brand's photos.
3. It sets the headline and the logo in code, in the brand's colours and fonts, where they read clearly. A face is never covered.
4. It reads the result back. A headline that does not match your words is drawn again or not used.

Pictures stay editable: open any of them in the [Studio](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/studio/).

## Image credits

| | Hobby | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Image credits a month | 75 | 300 | 3,000 |

A picture's price in credits follows the model and the size, and shows before you make it. A picture that fails is given back. A top-up adds 100 credits for $8, and top-up credits roll over. On paid plans, spare allowance moves between generations and image credits in Plan & billing: 2 generations make 1 image credit, up to 10 moves a month.

**Automatic** picks the model for each picture. You can pick one yourself; each model picker shows what it is good for (faces, products, words or none).

## Steer one piece

**For this piece**, on the Blog and Social pages, takes what you want for this piece's pictures in your own words. Pick one of the brand's own photos, or add reference images marked as style, person or product, and say where each is used: the header, the sections or one channel. The price shows before it generates.

## The brand's rule for AI pictures

**Brands › Look › Rules and facts** sets when AI may make pictures for the brand: when a piece asks, for every picture in the brand's style, only the header, or never. With never, an article's header is a credited stock photo or brand art, and a post takes only the brand's own photo. Asking for one picture yourself, in the panel or the Studio, still makes it.

Pictures of people show clear adults only. A brand whose offer is about children is never offered made people.

## When something goes wrong

- **No picture was offered for a post.** The brand needs a kept look first. Keep one under **Brands › Look**.
- **The words on a picture are wrong.** The read-back catches most; open it in the Studio and edit the text layer.
- **Not enough credits.** Buy a top-up or move allowance in Plan & billing.
