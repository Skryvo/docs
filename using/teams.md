# Teams and roles

> Invite people to work in your account with a role and chosen brands, with no seats to buy.

A team works in its owner's account: the same brands, channels, calendar and library. Pro and Enterprise invite as many people as they like, with no seats to buy; everything the team makes uses the owner's plan.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings › Team** and invite by email, with a role and the brands they may reach.
2. They get a link that works once, for 7 days, and only for that email address.
3. They join free, with a new account or the one they have.

Resending makes a new link and stops the old one; an invite can be withdrawn.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including the plan and billing. One per workspace. |
| Admin | Members and invites (editors and reviewers), brands, channels, automations and approval settings; writes, schedules and approves. No billing. |
| Editor | Writes, edits, designs and schedules on their brands. On a brand that needs approval, their posts wait. Cannot approve. |
| Reviewer | Reads their brands and approves posts or asks for changes. Changes nothing else. |

Nobody grants a role above their own. Only the owner adds, changes or removes admins. A member can reach all brands, which includes brands added later, or chosen ones; they then see only those brands, their channels, posts and drafts.

## Working in several teams

A person can belong to several workspaces. The workspace switcher in the sidebar shows for people in more than one. Each person also keeps their own workspace.

## When the plan lapses

If the owner's plan is no longer Pro or Enterprise, the workspace turns read-only for members: they still read everything, and every change is refused until the owner renews. Nothing is deleted.

## Leaving

Removing a member, or a member leaving, keeps everything they made. Their API keys and agent sign-ins for that workspace stop at once.

## Agents in a team

A key acts in the workspace it was made in, with its maker's role and brands. An agent sign-in acts in the workspace picked on the consent page. A reviewer's agent can only read, and changing a brand's look needs an admin. See [Safety and approvals](https://skryvo.ai/docs/agents/safety/).
