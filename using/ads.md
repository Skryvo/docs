# Ad copy

> Write ad copy for search and social ad platforms, held to each platform's limits and checked by Ad-Ready.

The ad writer makes ad copy in the brand's voice for each ad platform you pick, in several variations, each held to that platform's limits. Skryvo writes the copy; it runs no ad.

## Write ads

1. Paste a product page address, and Skryvo reads its name, what it is, its benefits (up to 5), its offer, its proof and up to 10 images. Or pick a saved brief, or type it.
2. **Improve** rewrites the description and suggests platforms, a goal, a style and the points to lead with. Each change shows before and after, and nothing moves until you choose **Apply**.
3. Pick the platforms: Google responsive search ads, Meta, TikTok in-feed, LinkedIn sponsored content and X. Pick the goal, 1 to 6 variations (3 by default) and the language.
4. Generate. One run, every platform and every variation, uses one generation, given back on failure.

## What you get

A tab per platform. Each variation has its angle and every line with a live count against its limit, previews with the real product image, and the **Ad-Ready** card. Google ads export as a CSV for Google Ads Editor.

## Ad-Ready

| Check | Fails when |
|---|---|
| Character limits | A line is over its platform's limit |
| Platform rules | Emoji, "!" in a headline, repeated punctuation, all capitals, "click here", phone numbers |
| Distinct | A variation repeats another |
| Claims | A proof, deadline, offer or claim the brief does not back |
| Hook | Meta's opening sentence runs past the 40 characters shown |
| Banned phrases | Tired AI phrases, or words the brief says never to use |

A variation is ready when it breaks no limit and no platform rule. Fixes are free and checked against the brief again.

## When something goes wrong

- **A fix will not run.** Fixes check against the brief the ad was written from; without one they refuse.
- **The product page could not be read.** Type the brief instead.
