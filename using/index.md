# Using Skryvo

> Every part of the app, from a brand's voice to the results a post earns.

Every part of the app, from a brand's voice to the results a post earns.

- [Brands and their voice (`Brands and voice`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/brands-and-voice/): How Skryvo reads a brand once, its voice, look and facts, and how every piece is then written in it.
- [Social posts (`Posts`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/posts/): Write a post for several platforms at once in the brand's voice, or write it yourself for free, then check and fix it.
- [Researched articles (`Articles`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/articles/): Plan an article from live search results, answer what only you know, then let Skryvo write, check and score it.
- [How a claim is checked (`Fact-checking`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/): How Skryvo checks the claims in an article against their sources, and what each verdict on the Fact Check card means.
- [Pictures and image credits (`Pictures`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/pictures/): How Skryvo designs pictures in a brand's kept look, what they cost in image credits, and how to steer one piece.
- [The Studio editor (`Studio`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/studio/): Edit any picture as layers: words, logo, photos and sizes, with AI changes priced before they run.
- [The calendar (`Calendar`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/calendar/): Plan, move and repeat posts on one calendar in your time zone, with posting times, previews and an AI plan.
- [Publishing and the live check (`Publishing`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/publishing/): How a scheduled post goes out on time, how Skryvo checks it went live, and what happens when it cannot.
- [Approvals](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/approvals/): When a brand's posts need approval, editors' posts wait for a reviewer, an admin or the owner to approve them.
- [Client review from a link (`Client review`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/client-review/): Send a client a link to approve, ask for changes on, or comment on posts, with no account and no sign-in.
- [Results and best times (`Results`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/results/): How Skryvo reads each published post at fixed checkpoints, finds what worked and suggests when to post.
- [Teams and roles (`Teams`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/teams/): Invite people to work in your account with a role and chosen brands, with no seats to buy.
- [Marketing emails (`Emails`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/emails/): Write a marketing email in the brand's voice, with subject lines, a preheader and a Send-Ready score.
- [Ad copy (`Ads`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/ads/): Write ad copy for search and social ad platforms, held to each platform's limits and checked by Ad-Ready.
- [Product listings (`Listings`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/listings/): Write a product listing shaped for its marketplace's rules, graded for compliance, facts, completeness and pull.
- [Repurposing a piece (`Repurposing`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/repurposing/): Turn an article or post into native posts for other platforms and a newsletter, in the brand's voice.
- [Bring your own article in (`Import an article`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/import-article/): Bring an article you wrote into Skryvo by paste, file or link, free and with no AI, then check, publish or repurpose it.
- [AI answers about your brand (`AI answers`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/ai-answers/): See whether AI answer engines name your brand to buyers, who they name instead, and which of your facts they get wrong.
