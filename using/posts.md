# Social posts

> Write a post for several platforms at once in the brand's voice, or write it yourself for free, then check and fix it.

The **Social** page writes one post per platform from one topic, each shaped for its own feed, in the brand's voice. Or write the words yourself, free and with no AI.

## Two ways to start

| | Write with AI | Write it yourself |
|---|---|---|
| What you give | A topic and the platforms | Your own words |
| What you get | One native post per platform | Your words on every channel you pick |
| Cost | One generation for all the platforms in the run | Free, no generation |
| Refund | A run where every platform fails is given back | Nothing to refund |

## Write with AI

1. Open **Social**, type the topic and say what matters: the news, the offer, who it is for.
2. Pick the platforms from the row of platform icons. A tick marks each picked one; you can write for a platform before its account is connected.
3. Set tone, audience, hashtags, call to action, language and whether to add a picture, or apply a preset.
4. Generate. Each post opens in the composer: you write on the left and see the post as its feed shows it on the right.

A **preset** keeps a recipe: platforms, the account to post as on each, the writing settings, pictures on or off, and comments to add under each post. Pin one and every new post for that brand starts from it.

## Write it yourself

Choose **Write it yourself** under the Social header. The composer opens on **Every channel**: what you type there goes to every channel you picked, and the count runs against the strictest one. Open a channel's own tab and start typing to give that channel its own words; **Use the shared words** puts it back. Pictures and a video added on Every channel go to every channel; added on one channel's tab, they go to that channel only.

## Score and fixes

Each AI post is scored on five things: engagement (a specific opening), clarity, search words and hashtags, the call to action, and fit for the platform. A post scoring 70 or more with nothing marked poor reads as ready.

Each issue has a **Fix** button. A fix rewrites only the part the issue is in, such as the first sentence or the last line, and the rewrite is kept only if it adds no claim, keeps every figure and link, and scores higher. Otherwise the fix says it could not do it without changing your facts and leaves the post as it was.

## The claim check

A post carries no links, so it is checked against what the writer was given: your brief, the brand's facts and the search facts for the topic. A figure, a rating, a guarantee, a safety claim, a customer story, a deadline or a product feature that none of those back is removed before you see the post. See [How a claim is checked](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/).

## Limits

Each platform's limits (characters, pictures, video) are on its channel page, for example [LinkedIn](https://skryvo.ai/docs/channels/linkedin/) or [X](https://skryvo.ai/docs/channels/x/). Posts to X also count against the plan's monthly X allowance; a post with a link counts as 10.

## When something goes wrong

- **Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest will not schedule.** They need a picture or a video. Add one and the post can be scheduled.
- **A post is over the limit.** The Fix for length cuts whole sentences between the opening and the ask, never one that holds a figure.
- **The generation was used and nothing came back.** A run where every platform fails is refunded.
