# Product listings

> Write a product listing shaped for its marketplace's rules, graded for compliance, facts, completeness and pull.

The **Product description** page writes a listing in the brand's voice, shaped for its marketplace: title and other titles, short and long descriptions, bullets, an SEO title, a meta description and search keywords.

## Write one

1. Pick the marketplace: Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, TikTok Shop, Google Shopping, WooCommerce, a SaaS product or a landing page.
2. Give the product's name, an overview in your words and its key features. Add the category, price, specifications, benefits, photos, keywords, audience and tone where you have them.
3. Generate. One listing uses one generation, given back if the run fails.

Exact details such as materials, capacity, ingredients and dimensions are read from your features in code and shown as "From your features".

## The Listing Grade

| Part | Weight | What it checks |
|---|---|---|
| Compliant | 35% | Field limits, banned characters, shouting, promotions and claims, under the marketplace's rules |
| Factual | 25% | Each figure matches one you or the brand's facts gave |
| Complete | 20% | How many of the details shoppers check for this category are answered |
| Persuasive | 20% | Benefits, specifics and variety between bullets |

A listing reads ready at 85 or more; one that breaks a marketplace rule is blocked and capped at 60. What can be fixed in code (length, shouting, repeats, banned characters in the title) is fixed before you see it, and each fix is listed.

Promotions such as "Best Seller" or "Free Shipping" are removed. A claim the writer made that you never gave is removed; a claim from your own facts, such as "FDA approved", is flagged for you to decide. A product name such as "Water Repellent" is not a claim.

A Complete score under 100 is honest when you have not given those details: the card asks for them instead of inventing them.

## Free listing tools

**Title Rescue** cleans and shortens up to 200 titles at a time, or a CSV, to a marketplace's limits. **Catalog Audit** checks a catalogue. Both are under Tools and use no generation.
