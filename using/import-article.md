# Bring your own article in

> Bring an article you wrote into Skryvo by paste, file or link, free and with no AI, then check, publish or repurpose it.

**Write it yourself** on the Blog page, or **Bring an article** in the Library, opens your own article in Skryvo's editor. It runs no AI and uses no generation.

## Ways in

| Way | What it takes |
|---|---|
| Blank | Start typing |
| Paste | HTML or Markdown |
| File | Word, Markdown, HTML or text, up to 10 MB |
| Link | A public page's address; its readable part is kept |
| My site | One of the brand's own blog posts |

Headings, lists, tables, links and up to 30 pictures (8 MB each) come with it. An article over 60,000 characters is refused, never cut. Before it opens, a receipt says what came in and what was left out, such as embeds or pictures over the limit. Reading it saves nothing and spends nothing.

Tick **This is mine to publish** to open it.

## Monthly limit

| | Hobby | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Articles brought in a month | 50 | 200 | 500 |

One counts per article you save, given back if the save fails.

## What you can do with it

The editor's strip offers:

- **Check SEO**, free.
- **Check my facts**, free: the claim check, on your words.
- **Add pictures**, from 1 image credit each.
- **Make posts**, one generation: posts for other platforms, as in [Repurposing](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/repurposing/).
- **Learn my voice from this**: adds the article to the brand's voice samples; switching it off withdraws it.

Then publish it like any article: see [Publishing](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/publishing/).

## An article from a live address

An article brought in from a link or from **My site** already lives somewhere. It goes to a blog only as a draft, or as an update of that same post.

## When something goes wrong

- **The link could not be read.** The page may need a sign-in or load its words by script. Paste the article instead.
- **A picture did not come with it.** The receipt names it; add it in the editor.

## From an agent

[`import_article`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/import-article/) brings an article in from text or a public address, after the person says it is theirs to publish. Every article tool then works on it.
