# Recipes

> Whole jobs from start to finish, each built only from what Skryvo does today.

Whole jobs from start to finish, each built only from what Skryvo does today.

- [A release note becomes a campaign (`Release note to campaign`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/release-note-campaign/): One release page becomes a researched article, a post per channel and a newsletter draft in the brand's voice.
- [A blog feed becomes posts (`Blog feed to posts`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/blog-feed-to-posts/): Each new article on your own blog becomes a post per channel in the brand's voice, scheduled after a yes.
- [A week planned by an agent (`A week by an agent`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/week-planned-by-agent/): An agent plans a week of posts for a brand, keeps the rows you choose, and schedules what you approve.
- [Client approval from a link (`Client approval link`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/client-approval-link/): Send a client one link to approve, ask for changes on, or comment on posts, with no account needed.
- [Your own video on several channels (`Your video, everywhere`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/own-video-everywhere/): Upload one video of your own and post it to several channels, each with its own words and settings.
- [One article on WordPress and Dev.to (`WordPress, then Dev.to`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/article-to-wordpress-and-devto/): Publish an article to your WordPress site first, then to Dev.to with a canonical link back to it.
- [A weekly results report (`Weekly results report`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/weekly-results-report/): Every Monday, a script reads how last week's posts and articles did and sends the numbers where your team reads.
- [A product launch kit (`Product launch kit`)](https://skryvo.ai/docs/recipes/product-launch-kit/): A marketplace listing, ad copy, a launch email and launch posts for one product, all in the brand's voice.
