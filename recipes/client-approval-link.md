# Client approval from a link

> Send a client one link to approve, ask for changes on, or comment on posts, with no account needed.

## What you get

A page your client opens with no sign-in. It shows the brand's name, logo and colour and the posts you chose, each with **Approve**, **Ask for changes** (a note is needed) and **Comment**. It never shows a member's name or email, a role or the plan.

## What it costs

Nothing.

## Steps

1. Pick the posts: from the Desk, a post's **Share with client** on the calendar, or, in a team, tick waiting posts on **Approvals**. One link holds 1 to 30 drafted, unpublished posts of one brand.
2. Choose how long it lasts: 1, 3, 7 or 14 days. The link is copied, and you send it yourself.
3. The client answers each post once. Every answer reaches you in the app and by email, with a link to the post's day on the calendar.
4. A post the client asked to change is held with their words. Fix it and send it for approval again; the same link then takes a new answer.

## What the answer does

| Who made the link | The client's answer |
|---|---|
| Someone who decides approvals (owner, admin or reviewer) | Is the team's decision: an approved post goes out at its time, a post sent back is held with the note. |
| An editor, on a brand that needs approval | Is kept beside the post, and a reviewer still decides. |
| Anyone, on a post outside approval | A yes clears it, a no holds it with the client's words. |

Comments decide nothing: up to 500 characters each, 20 per post.

## Ending a link

It ends when it expires, when its maker ends it or leaves the workspace, or when the team's plan lapses. An ended link tells the client to ask for a new one. Teams and approvals are described on [Approvals](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/approvals/) and [Client review](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/client-review/).

## With an agent

There is no tool that makes a client link; it is made in the app. In a team, an agent can decide a post waiting for approval with [`review_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/review-post/), as its person's own decision.
