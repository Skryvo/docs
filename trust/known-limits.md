# Known limits

> What Skryvo does not do today, which channels wait on a platform, and the limits a post can meet.

Everything here is true of Skryvo today. When one changes, this page and the [changelog](https://skryvo.ai/changelog/) change with it.

## Not built, or on hold

- **No AI video.** A post can carry a video you upload; Skryvo writes its caption, title and description. The Studio can turn a design into a 6-second MP4 whose layers slide into place, and that is all the motion it makes.
- **No shared inbox and no social listening.** Skryvo publishes and reads your own posts' results; it does not gather replies or mentions into one place.
- **Reddit** is on hold, and **Medium** is not built.

## Waiting on a platform

- **LinkedIn Page**: built, and waiting for the platform to approve Skryvo's app. Personal LinkedIn profiles post today.
- **First comments and replies on Facebook, Instagram and Threads** wait on Meta's review of the comment permissions. Posting and results on all three work today.
- **Tagging a LinkedIn company** by name needs LinkedIn's approval; until then the name goes out as plain text.

## Results Skryvo cannot read

- **Slack** answers "ok" and returns no post, so a Slack message is confirmed by Slack's answer, not read back, and has no numbers.
- **TikTok** views are not read: that needs a permission Skryvo has not asked for yet. Posting works.
- **Google Business Profile** gives no numbers per post.
- Poll votes on **Telegram** and **Discord** are not read.

## Limits a post can meet

- **X** posts are a monthly allowance (Hobby 30, Pro 100, Enterprise 1500), and a post with a link counts as 10. At the limit, X posts stop with a message and other channels keep posting.
- An agent's [`write_post`](https://skryvo.ai/docs/tools/write-post/) writes for up to 4 platforms at a time, from a topic of up to 200 characters.
- **Pinterest** needs a board for every pin. **TikTok** needs the audience you choose for each post; a post without one is held.
- An **Instagram** story is one picture or clip, with no caption.
- **Google Business Profile** may refuse an update with a phone number in its words; use the Call now button instead.
- **YouTube** videos cannot be added to a playlist yet.
- Each platform has its own video size, length and format limits, and Skryvo holds a video a platform would refuse before it is sent, with the reason. Each [channel page](https://skryvo.ai/docs/channels/) has the numbers.
- An **article read from a live address** goes to a blog only as a draft, or as an update of that same WordPress post.
- After a trial ends with no card, the account is read-only until a card is added: everything stays to see and export, and nothing new is made or published.

## Articles

- Writing a researched article takes about four minutes. One that stops partway is reported as failed and not charged.
- The fact check reads each cited sentence against its source. It reports what it found, and a claim no source states cannot be verified. See [How a claim is checked](https://skryvo.ai/docs/using/fact-checking/).
