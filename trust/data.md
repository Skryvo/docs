# Your data

> What Skryvo stores, where it lives, who else handles it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

This page summarises the [Privacy Policy](https://skryvo.ai/privacy/), which is the binding text.

## What Skryvo stores

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| Account | Your name, email address and profile picture. |
| What you create | Topics, briefs, brand voices, drafts, posts, articles, emails, pictures, files you upload, your calendar and your feedback on each result. |
| Your brand | What Skryvo read from your website's pages and the public posts of the social accounts you added, to learn the voice, facts and look. It reads public pages only. |
| Connected channels | The access tokens each platform gave, encrypted with Skryvo's own key. |
| Keys and sign-ins | API keys and app tokens, stored only as hashes. |
| Billing | Your plan, its renewal date and your invoices. Stripe holds the card. |

## Where it lives

The database, sign-in and file storage run on Google Cloud and Firebase. The API runs on Render. skryvo.ai and app.skryvo.ai are hosted by Hostinger. AI models are reached through OpenRouter and, for some writing and picture steps, directly; the Privacy Policy lists every company that handles data and why.

## What Skryvo never does

- It never sells your data and never uses it for advertising.
- It does not train AI models on your content and does not let anyone else do so through it.
- It publishes only what you, or an agent you approved, scheduled.

## How long it is kept

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and content | As long as you have the account, unless you delete it sooner. |
| A connected channel's token | Until you disconnect it, which deletes the token at once. |
| Free tools on skryvo.ai | Free articles and pictures are deleted 30 days after they are made. An email pasted to reply to is not kept after the reply. |
| A webhook delivery | Its event's body, for 30 days, so the deliveries route can show it. |
| Payment records | As long as tax law requires, even after the account is deleted. |

## Export and delete

You can export your content at any time. Delete your account from Settings: it closes after 30 days, which you can cancel until then, and after that your profile, content, brand voices and files are deleted. You can also ask by email at support@skryvo.ai. How to remove Skryvo's access on a platform you connected: [Data deletion](https://skryvo.ai/data-deletion/).
